7. Majlis credentials

Majlis is reached with a credential issued to a named board member. A member's credential is how a decision is attributed to them, and a vote that cannot be attributed is not a record of anything — which is the whole reason the application exists.

  • Keep your credential to yourself. Do not share it, and do not let someone act under it.
  • Tell us immediately if you think it has been compromised, and it will be reissued.
  • A credential may be withdrawn at any time when someone leaves a board, or where it is being misused.
  • Nothing recorded in Majlis signs anything on chain. It records what a board decided; it does not execute it.
