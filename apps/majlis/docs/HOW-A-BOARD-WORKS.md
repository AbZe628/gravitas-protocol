# How a Shariah board actually works, and what Majlis owes it

Written **9 September 2026**. Majlis was built from the outside — from what the
software ought to be. This is the other direction: what the job is, what a
supervisor obliges a board to produce, and whether Majlis has an answer for each
obligation or does not.

Every obligation below is quoted from a binding text with its paragraph number,
so a claim in this file can be checked against the source rather than believed.
Where Majlis has no answer it says so.

---

## 1. Which texts actually bind a board

There is no single global rulebook, and this matters more than it sounds: it is
the reason Majlis is right to ship no standard of its own.

| body | text | force |
|---|---|---|
| **Bank Negara Malaysia** | Shariah Governance Policy Document, BNM/RH/PD 028-100, issued 20 September 2019 | **binding** on Malaysian IFIs. `S` paragraphs are requirements; non-compliance may result in enforcement action |
| **CBUAE** | Standard re. Shari'ah Governance for Islamic Financial Institutions, and the Higher Shariah Authority | binding in the UAE |
| **AAOIFI** | Governance standards: **GS 1 (2024)** Shari'ah Governance Framework, **GS 19** SSB Appointment and Composition, **GS 20** SSB Functions and Operations, **GS 21** SSB Review and Report, **GS 22** subsidiaries, **GS 11** Internal Shari'ah Audit (supersedes GS 3 Internal Shari'ah Review), **GS 8** Central Shari'ah Board | binding **only where a jurisdiction or an institution adopts them** |
| **IFSB** | guiding principles | guidance |

### A correction to a claim this project has been repeating

**AAOIFI Shariah Standard No. 62 is still a draft.** It was exposed in November
2023, the feedback deadline was extended twice to 31 July 2024, and as of the
most recent public position it remains in draft and under revision. No final
text and no implementation date have been issued.

It is also **about sukuk**, not about contract templates generally: its central
proposal is that legal title, not merely beneficial ownership, must transfer to
the issuing SPV.

The line this project has carried — *"AAOIFI Standard 62 (2023) obliges
institutions to re-audit existing contract templates"* — is therefore not true
today, in two ways at once. It appears in the rebuild brief as an established
market fact. **Any use of it in the site, the investor material, or a document
sent to a bank should be removed or rewritten**, because a bank's own counsel
will know it is a draft, and one wrong claim of that kind costs the credibility
of the true ones beside it.

What is defensible: *if* SS 62 is finalised and adopted in a jurisdiction, it
would force a re-papering of sukuk structures, and boards would need to
re-examine documentation they have already approved. That is a conditional, and
it should be written as one.

## 2. What a supervisor obliges a board to produce

From the BNM policy document, which is the most specific of the binding texts
and freely published. Paragraph numbers are as issued.

### Meetings

| | |
|---|---|
| **S 11.2** | the committee must meet **at least once in every two months** |
| **S 11.3** | an Islamic window or foreign branch: at least **twice a year** |
| **S 11.4** | a member must attend **at least 75%** of meetings each financial year, and **must not send a substitute** |
| **S 11.5** | attendance other than in person must remain **the exception rather than the norm** |
| **S 11.6** | the number of meetings and **every member's attendance** must be disclosed in the annual report |
| **S 11.7** | quorum requires **a majority of Shariah qualified members present** |
| **S 11.8** | a decision is **by simple majority** |
| **S 11.9** | a member with a conflict must **excuse himself and abstain** on that matter |

### The record

| | |
|---|---|
| **S 11.14** | *"clear and accurate minutes ... to record the decisions or advice of the Shariah committee, including the key deliberations, rationale for each decision or advice made, and any significant concerns and dissenting views"* |
| **S 10.7** | where the committee adds restrictions when applying a central ruling, the institution must document the deliberations and justifications, obtain the board's view, and **notify the supervisor immediately** |
| **S 10.8** | any record submitted to the supervisor, including a rectification plan, must be **supported with key deliberations, rationale, and any significant concerns or dissenting views** |
| **S 10.9(e)** | where the committee delegates, **sufficient records of the discussion leading to the decision** must be maintained |

### Access to information

| | |
|---|---|
| **S 11.10** | the institution must give the committee access to all information required, **timely, accurate, comprehensive and clear** |
| **S 11.11** | access to **third-party expert advice**, at the institution's cost |
| **S 11.12** | if access or expert advice is **declined, denied or withheld**, anyone who knows — including a committee member — **must inform the board**, which must rectify it |
| **S 11.13** | the board and management **must not exercise undue influence** on the committee's judgement |

### The four control functions, and who they report to

**S 16.3** requires all three of Shariah risk management, Shariah review and
Shariah audit; research sits with the secretariat.

| function | duty | reports to |
|---|---|---|
| **Shariah risk management** (S 17.2) | identify, assess, monitor and report Shariah non-compliance risk | board, committee, management |
| **Shariah review** (S 18.2) | regular assessment of compliance of operations; **report regularly** on findings; keep the committee updated on new rulings | board, committee, management |
| **Shariah audit** (S 19.2) | independent assessment; an audit **methodology**, an audit **plan**, documented audit **programmes**, and a report carrying **findings, recommendations, the auditee's response and its action plan** | board and committee |

### Non-compliance

The committee's own duties (**S 10.2**) include *"deliberating and affirming a
Shariah non-compliance finding by any relevant functions"* and *"endorsing a
rectification measure"*. Under **S 10.5** a matter affecting the safety and
soundness of the institution must go to the board **immediately**.

### The annual opinion — the most prescriptive thing in the document

| | |
|---|---|
| **S 22.1(b)** | the committee must disclose its responsibilities and **its opinion on the state of the institution's compliance** |
| **S 22.6** | the disclosure must be **signed by not less than two members** |
| **S 22.7** | the institution must have a **written policy, endorsed by the committee and approved by the board**, for how the committee derives that opinion |
| **G 22.8** | that policy may cover planning and **materiality**, obtaining evidence, consultation, forming the opinion, and how it is published |
| **S 22.5** | the disclosure must not be false, misleading, inaccurate or **incomplete** |

**S 22.9 gives the opinion two permitted forms and no others:**

> (a) nothing has come to the Shariah committee's attention that causes the
> Shariah committee to believe that the operations, business, affairs and
> activities of the IFI involve any material Shariah non-compliances; or
>
> (b) the overall operations ... are in compliance with Shariah but it has come
> to the Shariah committee's attention that a material Shariah non-compliance
> event(s) has occurred and has been or in the process of being rectified.

And **S 22.10**: where it is (b), the disclosure must state the **nature, status
and measures undertaken** for each event.

## 3. Majlis against each obligation

Checked against the running application on 9 September 2026, not against the
source. *Reachable* means opened in the browser under a credential.

| obligation | Majlis today | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| meet every two months (S 11.2) | meetings exist with dates; **nothing measures the interval or warns when it lapses** | **gap** |
| attendance per member (S 11.4, S 11.6) | `PUT /meetings/:id/attendance` records it | **has it, reachable** |
| 75% attendance rule | not computed anywhere | **gap** |
| quorum is a majority of qualified members (S 11.7) | quorum is modelled and enforced on votes | **has it** |
| simple majority (S 11.8) | thresholds are the board's own, set per direction | **has it, and better** — it lets a board be stricter than the floor |
| conflict: excuse and abstain (S 11.9) | **no conflict-of-interest field, no abstention-for-conflict, no register of interests** | **gap, and it is a named obligation** |
| minutes with deliberations, rationale, concerns **and dissent** (S 11.14) | deliberation, reasoning on each vote, and dissent that *"carries words or it is not recorded"* | **has it, and it is the strongest part of the product** |
| dissent shown as prominently as the majority | committee reports show dissent at the same size | **has it** |
| record supporting a submission to the supervisor (S 10.8) | the matter pack assembles exactly this | **has it, reachable** |
| information access, timely and complete (S 11.10) | the pack and the board book | **has it** |
| **access declined must be reported (S 11.12)** | **nothing** — there is no way for a member to record that the institution withheld something | **gap** |
| third-party expert advice (S 11.11) | committees can be formed; **an external expert is not a thing Majlis models** | **partial** |
| Shariah risk management reporting (S 17.2) | not modelled | **gap** |
| Shariah review reporting (S 18.2) | examinations cover this | **has it** |
| Shariah audit: methodology, plan, programme, findings, **auditee response and action plan** (S 19.2) | examinations record the period, **how the sample was chosen**, coverage, and one finding per condition. **The auditee's response and action plan are not captured** | **partial** |
| affirming an SNC finding, endorsing rectification (S 10.2) | the nine-step incident flow does exactly this | **has it, reachable** |
| immediate escalation to the board (S 10.5) | no notion of immediacy or escalation | **gap** |
| annual opinion, one of two prescribed forms (S 22.9) | the annual report **leaves the opinion paragraph empty on purpose** | see below |
| signed by **at least two** members (S 22.6) | signing exists; **no minimum of two is enforced on the annual report** | **gap** |
| nature, status and measures per event (S 22.10) | **`GET /disclosure` already returns exactly this** — and no screen opens it | **built and unreachable** |
| written policy for deriving the opinion (S 22.7) | not modelled | **gap** |

### The empty opinion paragraph — right instinct, wrong execution

Majlis leaves the annual opinion blank because *"it is the only part of the
document that belongs to the board"*. The instinct is correct and should be
kept. But a supervisor has already reduced the choice to **two sentences**, and
a blank line is not the same as a choice.

The honest form is to offer the board those two forms of words, with S 22.9
quoted beside them, and let the board pick one and edit it — and to say in place
that these are Bank Negara's words and apply to a Malaysian institution, because
Majlis names no standard. That is still the board deciding. It is not Majlis
concluding anything.

### The three gaps most worth closing

1. **Conflict of interest.** S 11.9 is explicit, every jurisdiction has a
   version of it, and Majlis has nothing: no register of interests, no way to
   declare one on a matter, no abstention that is recorded as *conflicted*
   rather than as *did not vote*. For a board portal this is table stakes.
2. **Withheld information.** S 11.12 makes a denied request a reportable event.
   Majlis is the natural place to record it and does not.
3. **The auditee's response.** An examination without the institution's answer
   and action plan is half of what S 19.2 asks for, and it is the half that
   makes the next examination possible.

## 4. The month, as it is actually lived

Assembled from the governance texts and from what the control functions are
required to do between meetings. This is the shape Majlis should fit.

| when | what happens | who | where it lives today in a real bank |
|---|---|---|---|
| ~3 weeks before | the secretariat collects what needs a decision: new products, changed documentation, questions from desks, findings from review | Shariah secretariat | email and a shared drive |
| ~2 weeks before | papers drafted; each item gets a memo with the structure, the question, and the secretariat's research | secretariat, Shariah research | Word |
| ~1 week before | the pack is circulated | secretariat | email attachments, sometimes a portal |
| the meeting | items taken in order; deliberation; decisions by majority; dissent noted | the committee | a room, minutes typed in Word |
| after | minutes drafted, circulated, corrected, adopted at the next meeting; resolutions written up as fatwas and signed | secretariat | Word, wet or scanned signatures |
| continuously | Shariah review tests transactions against approved structures; findings raised | review function | **spreadsheets** |
| continuously | Shariah audit runs its plan; reports findings with management responses | internal audit | audit software the Shariah team often cannot use |
| on an event | an SNC is found, affirmed by the committee, rectified, and income purified | everyone | email and a spreadsheet |
| annually | the committee's report and opinion for the annual report | committee | Word |

**Where the work really lives is the answer to what Majlis replaces.** Minutes,
resolutions, the pack, the register of what was approved, and the purification
ledger are Word, email and Excel today, and that is exactly the set Majlis
already holds. Shariah audit is the one function that usually has real software
and does not need replacing — it needs a way to send its findings *in*.

## 5. What this changes about the product

1. **Stop asserting SS 62.** Rewrite it as a conditional wherever it appears.
2. **Add conflict of interest.** It is the largest named obligation with no
   answer at all, and it is cheap: a declaration on a member, a declaration on a
   matter, and an abstention that records the reason.
3. **Open `GET /disclosure`.** It already returns what S 22.10 requires.
4. **Offer the two forms of the annual opinion**, quoted and attributed, instead
   of a blank.
5. **Record the auditee's response** on an examination.
6. **Record a refusal of information.** One button, one record, one line in the
   pack.
7. **Count the interval between meetings** and say when it has lapsed. Majlis
   already computes review intervals; this is the same machinery.

Nothing here asks Majlis to conclude anything. Each is a place where the board
does the deciding and the product currently has nowhere to put the answer.
