# Concept

The full concept document — including everything not built in Stage One — is
[`CONCEPT.pdf`](CONCEPT.pdf) in this directory.

Summary of what it covers:

1. The problem: a rule with operational force makes the process of approving it the most sensitive
   part of the system
2. The governing principle: the vote is the signature
3. Roles: scholar, chair, technical liaison (no vote, no key), institution administrator, auditor
4. The life of a decision, end to end
5. The asymmetry between restricting and permitting
6. The assistant and its four structural constraints
7. The standing brief on technological change
8. Key management, succession, absence and rotation
9. Difference of opinion between boards
10. What accumulates: rulings joined to reasoning, mechanism, parameters and effect
11. Practical requirements: language, devices, notification, audit export, continuity
12. What it is not
13. How it would be built — four stages
14. Open questions
