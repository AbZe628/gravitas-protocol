Treatment of a tangible asset ratio breached by drift rather than by act

The question put to the board

The rule in force suspends secondary trading the moment the tangible ratio falls below the threshold, and provides nothing further. In practice a pool may cross it through ordinary market movement, with no act of the manager, and be restored within days.

Held outside this question
Whether the 51% threshold itself is correct. Not reopened here.
Whether redemption at net asset value may continue. It may; already settled.
What these terms will do checked before every transaction

Read by the Gravitas Policy Registry before every transaction that depends on them. A transaction that would breach one does not execute — refused at the point of attempt rather than found afterwards. There is no interval to drift in.
