The whole application

Twenty-three screens, four doors.

A question arrives, the board decides it, the decision governs the bank, and someone checks the bank obeyed. That is the whole of Shariah governance, and it is the whole of this product. Every screen already built sits under exactly one of these four. Nothing was removed and nothing new has to be learned.

The board portals corporate directors already use — Diligent, Boardvantage, Convene — organise a board around the meeting: agenda, pack, minutes, resolutions. That is three of these four. A Shariah board has a fourth, because its resolutions do not end at the minute book: they bind the bank's trading afterwards, and somebody has to look. No product sold today covers all four.
