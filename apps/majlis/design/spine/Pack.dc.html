Suspension of leveraged index instruments

The whole matter, in the order a member reads it. Seven parts, one act at the end.
01
The question, as it was put
“May the treasury desk continue to hold index instruments whose exposure is obtained through borrowing, where the borrowing is internal to the index provider and not to us?”
Rakbank Treasury · Y. Al-Mansouri · 14 August · the board was told the same hour
02
What this board has already said about it
Two rulings in force touch this. 2024-009 permits index exposure where the index itself is screened. 2025-031 sets the debt limit at 30% of market value and says the board, not the provider, sets it.
Neither has been put to this question before. The board is deciding something new.

05
What follows if this passes
Four holdings leave the permitted register the moment the ruling takes effect. Two contract shapes in the library gain a clause. The desk is notified; nothing is executed by Majlis.

What Majlis could not tell you
