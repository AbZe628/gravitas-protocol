How this is ordered
Nothing is deleted and nothing is edited. A ruling that replaces another is written after it and says so. What stands is read by following that chain — never by taking the most recent date, which can lie.
What nobody can do here
Change a standard. That needs a ruling in force, not a control.
Remove a ruling. A correction is written; the original stays readable.
